# Islita v0.9.0

## 中文

- **桌面宠物走出灵动岛**：开启「在桌面上显示」后，宠物会以透明、无边框、始终置顶的独立窗口出现在桌面任意位置，不抢焦点、不占任务栏。默认允许自由散步，会沿着当前显示器的可用区域走动、停下、转身，靠近桌面边缘时自动回头；把它拖到半空松手，它会轻轻落回地面。
- **三套形象、完整的动作**：DeepSeek 大肥鱼的 Q 版与变身形态、Hachiware 都有站立、行走、睡觉、开心四组透明立绘（Q 版另有拖拽姿态）；Mochi 使用内置矢量形象。每张立绘为独立 WebP，切换姿势只换图，不做补间，任何缩放下都保持清晰。
- **随手可用的小互动**：单击是摸摸，拖动是搬家，右键打开紧凑菜单——喂饭、玩耍、休息、摸摸，一个「散步」开关（对应 `petRoam`），直达设置，以及「从桌面隐藏」。鼠标停在宠物上、菜单打开或它睡着了时，它会自动停下；精力过低或安静太久会打盹，互动后醒来。
- **说的话和灵动岛同步**：额度警告、临界、即将在重置前耗尽、服务商连不上、轮询暂停、任务完成，以及它自己饿了、困了、无聊了，都会用当前角色的口吻在宠物旁边的气泡里说一句；气泡在深色和浅色壁纸下都清晰可读。灵动岛面板和桌面宠物共用同一份状态，喂食、改名、换形象、换形态在两边即时同步。
- **只在本地**：立绘、状态和位置全部保存在本机；桌面宠物不联网，不读取任何凭据，也不上传图片。
- **可关闭、可回退**：`petOnDesktop` 默认关闭，`petRoam` 默认开启；关闭后窗口保持隐藏且不消耗动画，位置会在下次打开时恢复。开启系统「减少动态效果」时，窗口仍会移动，但装饰性的浮动、跳跃和摇摆会停用。

## English

- **The pet leaves the island**: with "Show on desktop" on, the pet appears anywhere on the desktop in its own transparent, frameless, always-on-top window that never steals focus and stays out of the taskbar. It wanders by default along the current monitor's work area, pausing and turning at random, turning back at the edges, and dropping gently to the floor if you let go of it in mid-air.
- **Three skins, full poses**: DeepSeek 大肥鱼 in both its chibi and transformed forms, and Hachiware each have stand, walk, sleep and happy transparent sprites (chibi also has a drag pose); Mochi keeps its built-in vector art. Each pose is its own WebP, swapped rather than tweened, so the art stays crisp at any display scale.
- **Small interactions, always at hand**: click to pat, drag to move, right-click for a compact menu — Feed, Play, Rest, Pet, a Wander toggle (`petRoam`), Settings, and Hide from desktop. It stops when the pointer is over it, a menu is open, or it is asleep; low energy or a long quiet spell puts it to sleep, and an interaction wakes it.
- **Speech stays in sync with the island**: quota warnings and critical levels, a window on pace to run out, an unreachable provider, paused polling, a finished task, and its own hunger, tiredness or boredom are spoken in the selected character's voice beside the pet, readable over dark and light wallpapers alike. The island panel and the desktop pet share one state, so feeding, renaming, skin and form changes appear on both immediately.
- **Local only**: art, state and position stay on the machine. The desktop pet makes no network request, reads no credentials and uploads nothing.
- **Opt-in and reversible**: `petOnDesktop` is off by default and `petRoam` is on; switching it off keeps the window hidden with no animation cost and restores its last position next time. With reduced motion enabled, the real movement stays but the decorative bob, hop and sway loops stop.
