# Islita v0.8.0

## 中文

- **桌面宠物来到灵动岛**：内置三套形象——原创的 Mochi、DeepSeek 大肥鱼和 Hachiware。DeepSeek 可以随时在常驻 Q 版与变身形态之间切换；喂食、玩耍、休息、摸摸会改变饱腹、心情和精力，互动累积经验并升级，离线衰减最多计算 12 小时。
- **宠物会看脸色说话**：额度进入警告或临界、窗口可能在重置前耗尽、服务商连不上、轮询暂停、任务完成，以及它自己饿了、困了、无聊了，都会用对应角色的口吻说一句。配额浮窗里也会出现一行头像加对白；灵动岛插件栏在事件发生时显示未读圆点。
- **导入自己的形象**：一张头像或 4:1 的四帧条带（PNG / WebP / GIF，最大 2 MB），只保存在本机，不上传。导入后仍会用当前选中形象的声音说话。
- **服务状态插件**：读取 Claude 与 OpenAI 官方 status page 的当前状态和最近事件，缓存 60 秒；一个页面不可用不影响另一个。
- **便签升级**：从一张草稿变成最多 20 张可切换的便签，每张 4000 字符，自动保存，旧版单张便签会自动迁移。
- **音乐升级**：可以选择本地音乐文件夹，递归扫描音频并自动配对同名 `.lrc` 歌词；记住上次的文件夹，浏览器预览里也能整目录导入。
- **OpenCode Go 月度额度**：30 天窗口作为第三个同心圆环显示——最外圈是月度，向内依次是每周和 5 小时；标签和颜色仍跟随使用比例最高的窗口。
- **灵动岛自动隐藏**：收起后只在屏幕顶部留一条细柄，鼠标移上去显示胶囊，停留片刻展开看板，离开后按设置里的延迟再次隐藏。
- **侧栏设置按钮**：环旁边新增一个齿轮按钮，直接打开仪表盘的设置页；可以在侧栏项目里关掉。
- **台词与素材说明**：内置的 DeepSeek 大肥鱼与 Hachiware 形象来自项目所有者提供的二创素材（已获商用授权）；对白是为这些角色原创的短句，不是作品原台词。

## English

- **A desktop pet moves into the island**: three bundled skins — the original Mochi, DeepSeek 大肥鱼 and Hachiware. DeepSeek switches at any time between its chibi and transformed forms. Feeding, playing, resting and patting change fullness, mood and energy; interactions earn XP and levels, and offline decay is capped at 12 hours.
- **The pet reads the room**: it speaks in the selected character's voice when a quota crosses a warning or critical threshold, a window is on pace to run out before it resets, a provider cannot be reached, polling is paused, a task finishes, or it is hungry, tired or bored. A ring's quota popover also gains a one-line avatar aside, and the island rail shows an unread dot when an event arrives.
- **Import your own sprite**: one portrait or a 4:1 four-frame strip (PNG / WebP / GIF, up to 2 MB), stored locally and never uploaded. The selected skin's voice still speaks for it.
- **Service status plugin**: the current indicator and recent incidents from the official Claude and OpenAI status pages, cached for 60 seconds; one page failing does not hide the other.
- **Sticky notes, plural**: up to 20 switchable notes of 4000 characters each, autosaved locally, with the 0.7 single note migrated automatically.
- **Music improvements**: choose a local music folder, scan it recursively and pair same-name `.lrc` lyrics automatically; the folder is remembered, and the browser preview can import a whole folder too.
- **OpenCode Go monthly quota**: the 30-day window is now the third concentric ring — monthly on the outside, weekly and 5-hour inside it; the label and colour still follow the highest-used window.
- **Island auto-hide**: the collapsed island leaves only a thin handle at the top of the screen; pointing at it reveals the capsule, resting on it expands the board, and it hides again after the configured delay.
- **Settings button on the bar**: a gear button next to the rings opens the dashboard's Settings tab directly and can be hidden with the other sidebar items.
- **Art and dialogue**: the bundled DeepSeek 大肥鱼 and Hachiware artwork comes from derivative material supplied by the project owner, who attests it is authorised for commercial use. The spoken lines are original short character-voice writing, not transcribed dialogue from the source works.
